# Hearth privacy policy

*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

Hearth is an Android home-screen launcher. It is free, open-source software
([source code](https://github.com/Aevrin/hearth)).

## The short version

Hearth collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to: it does not request
the internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server.

## What Hearth stores, and where

Everything Hearth keeps stays in its private storage on your phone:

- **Your settings** (grid size, colours, icon style, gestures and so on).
- **Your home screen layout**: which apps, shortcuts, folders and widgets are where.
- **Custom app names and icon choices** you set.
- **Notes and checklists** you type into a sidebar. They stay in Hearth's
  storage on the phone; they're included in backups you make yourself unless
  you switch that off in Settings → Backup.
- **How often you open each app**, used only to show the optional "Most used"
  row in the app drawer. This count never leaves the phone and is not included
  in backups.
- **Generated icon images**, cached so they load quickly. These are rebuilt
  from your apps' own icons and can be deleted at any time by clearing Hearth's
  cache.

Hearth reads the list of installed apps, their icons and their shortcuts so it
can show them. It does not record, share or transmit that list.

## Backups

**Settings → Backup** saves your settings and layout to a file **you choose**,
and restores from a file you pick. Hearth never uploads it anywhere, and it
opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup.

## Widgets and shortcuts

Widgets you add are drawn by the apps that provide them, under Android's widget
system; what those apps do is governed by their own privacy policies. The same
applies to app shortcuts you open from Hearth.

## Permissions

- **Request to delete packages**: lets Hearth show Android's uninstall prompt
  when you choose *Uninstall*. Nothing is uninstalled without your confirmation.
- **Expand status bar**: lets the swipe-down gesture open the notification shade.
- **Widget binding**: the first time you add a widget from an app, Android asks
  whether Hearth may create widgets. Hearth only uses this to show the widgets
  you add.

Hearth has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no tracking.

## Children

Hearth collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
